BUNDLE / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Version 2026-09-17. This is a product draft and must be reviewed and finalized for the jurisdictions in which Bundle operates before publication.

Information we collect

Bundle may collect account credentials, email address, phone number, profile information, profile media, approximate or device-provided location when permission is granted, verification status, subscription status, messages, reports, blocks, device/session information and consent records.

Identity verification

Bundle uses a specialist identity-verification provider for government ID/passport and liveness workflows. Bundle is designed to store verification status and provider references rather than unnecessarily storing raw identity documents.

How we use information

We use information to provide the service, verify accounts, operate discovery and messaging, process memberships, prevent fraud and abuse, moderate content, provide support, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We may use processors for authentication/OTP, identity verification, cloud storage, subscriptions, notifications, moderation, analytics and hosting. We do not sell personal information as a product.

Retention and deletion

Users can request deletion in the app or through the public deletion page. Certain information may need to be retained where required for legal, fraud-prevention, safety or financial-record purposes.

Your controls

Users can manage notification preferences, dating preferences, sessions, blocked users and account deletion from the Bundle app.
