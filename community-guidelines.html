Community Guidelines

Bundle is built for respectful, adult dating.

Do

Use truthful profile information, current photos, respectful language, clear intentions and consent. Report suspected scams, impersonation, threats or underage users.

Don't

No harassment, hate, threats, sexual exploitation, extortion, scams, spam, impersonation, non-consensual intimate content, or attempts to evade verification or moderation.

Moderation

Bundle may use automated safety screening and human review. Content may be rejected, accounts may be restricted, and decisions may be appealed through support.
